# Atmosfere — Creative Reference Link

Social Content Direction — Vol. 01

Built by Inloop Media

---

A single-page moodboard / social content direction site for **Atmosfere** — pitching two
properties in one CRL: **atmosfere.in** (Space & Consultancy) and **atmosfere.living** (the
living range). Brand identity (logo, warm-ivory theme, typography) taken from the Atmosfere
brand guidelines; content pillars and reference links supplied by Inloop Media.

- **Display type:** Cormorant Garamond (editorial match for Black Mango) · **Body:** Jost · **Labels:** DM Mono
- **Palette:** warm ivory `#F9F7F3` ground, white cards, Deep Earth `#816F66` accent, taupe / stone details, charcoal `#202020` type
- **Grid A — atmosfere.in · Space & Consultancy:** Transformation & Proof · Designing Education · Property Intelligence — 10 pieces
- **Grid B — atmosfere.living:** The Atmosfere Edit · Shop the Corner · Transformation & Proof — 12 pieces
- **22 reference pieces** total (reels, statics + carousels) with per-grid filters and a lightbox gallery
